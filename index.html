About

I am currently a research scientist at Google DeepMind, where I work on robotics safety and HRI. Previously, I was a Ph.D. student at the University of Southern California in the department of Political Science, and before that an undergraduate student at the Université de Montréal.

During my Ph.D., my research focused on the intersection of AI and society, and specifically how intelligent systems shape human behavior, cognition, and social organization—often in ways that contradict conventional assumptions.

In my book project, I am developing a theory of Human-AI-AI-Human interaction, examining what happens when people increasingly act through intelligent systems that interact with each other on our behalf.

This book serves as an attempt to understand the social and behavioral implications of artificial general intelligence (AGI), and the mass delegation of AI agents and emdodied models in our society.
